<objective>
Complete PATR-02/PATR-04: a full-screen detail view (placeholder hero, name, full tag list, plain ingredient-name list, makeable badge, conditional description and missing-ingredients sections) reachable by tapping any card in 03-02's grid, replacing that plan's `onSelect={() => {}}` stub with real navigation. Per D-44, the missing-ingredient detail surfaces ONLY here on the detail screen — `RecipeCard` (03-01) deliberately stays at the D-38 content level and never renders `missingCategoryNames`.

The critical architectural decision this plan makes explicit: the detail view fetches by `recipeId` via its OWN `useRecipeDetail` query, never by holding onto the already-fetched grid-row object — this is what lets 03-05's live-sync invalidation reach an open detail view (D-47) instead of leaving it showing a stale snapshot from tap-time (the exact bug `03-RESEARCH.md`'s Pitfall 1 warns about).

Purpose: closes the guest-facing loop this phase exists to deliver — a patron can now go from "browse the grid" to "see exactly why a drink isn't available right now."
Output: `useRecipeDetail.ts`; `RecipeDetail.tsx`; `RecipeBrowse.tsx` gains real tap-to-detail navigation.
</objective>
