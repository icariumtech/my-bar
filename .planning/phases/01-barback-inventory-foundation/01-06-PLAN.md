<objective>

Purpose: make the swipe interaction match what the owner actually asked for in D-08's "few seconds undo grace period" without breaking the WR-01/WR-02 race-condition fixes already shipped, and without the reveal layer ever showing the reserved in-stock accent color at rest.
Output: `swipeVisuals.ts` (pure, unit-tested color/surface derivation) and a redesigned `IngredientRow.tsx` swipe/undo/commit state machine.
